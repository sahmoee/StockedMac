## Description

Your kitchen command center, made for Mac.

Stocked for Mac brings your kitchen inventory, groceries, recipes, plans, and cooking history to a spacious desktop workspace. Organize a household, research new ideas, and prepare the week with the precision of a Mac app.

SEE THE WHOLE KITCHEN
Browse inventory, grocery needs, recipes, saved ideas, planned meals, history, and profile preferences without losing context.

PLAN ON A BIGGER CANVAS
Build a realistic week, check missing ingredients, review what can be cooked now, and turn plans into a focused shopping list.

IMPORT RECIPES WITH CONTROL
Add a direct recipe URL or explore supported publishers. StockedMac discovers recipe pages, parses structured content locally with its bundled parser, and lets you review imported details.

SYNC A STOCKED HOUSEHOLD
Connect the same household used by Stocked on iPhone to coordinate inventory, groceries, recipes, and plans across invited devices.

BUILT FOR SERIOUS RECIPE WORK
Run source health checks, inspect activity, cache supported content, export recipes to a spreadsheet, remove retired sources, and keep the library organized over time.

PRIVATE AND TRANSPARENT
Kitchen data is stored locally on your Mac. StockedMac has no advertising SDK and does not use information for cross-app tracking. Network activity occurs only for sources, household sync, support, and intelligent features you use.

Recipe content belongs to its publishers and is subject to their terms. Import only content you are authorized to use. Suggestions and nutrition estimates are informational and are not medical advice; verify allergies, dietary restrictions, and food-safety requirements yourself.
