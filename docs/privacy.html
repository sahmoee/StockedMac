Privacy Policy

StockedMac has no advertising SDK and does not use information for cross-app tracking or sell personal information.

Local kitchen data

Inventory, groceries, recipes, plans, history, profile preferences, source settings, images, caches, activity, and exports are stored on your Mac. A bundled Python parser processes supported recipe markup locally.

Server Mac discovery

An optional background service on your Server Mac rotates through enabled publishers and supported cuisine indexes and stages candidate recipe URLs. StockedMac remains the validation boundary: it parses candidates, requires a usable image and instructions, standardizes titles, converges duplicates, preserves provenance, and only then publishes approved recipes to the shared Stocked service.

Recipe sites and ownership

Discovery and imports download pages, feeds, sitemaps, and images from enabled sources. Publishers receive ordinary request metadata such as IP address, time, user agent, and URL under their policies. Stocked does not claim ownership of third-party recipes, photographs, editorial material, publisher names, or trademarks. Rights remain with their respective rights holders and content remains subject to each source's terms and license. Available source links and attribution are retained.

Household and service sync

When configured, household sync can send a household code, member/device identifier, display name, inventory, groceries, recipes, plans, changes, and deletion markers to the Stocked Cloudflare service. Operational request data may appear in Cloudflare observability.

Deletion

Use clear controls or remove StockedMac Application Support data to erase one Mac. This does not automatically erase a shared household or other devices. Request server deletion, a source correction, or content removal at support@sowensstudios.com.
