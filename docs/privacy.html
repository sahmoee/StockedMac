Privacy Policy

StockedMac has no advertising SDK and does not use information for cross-app tracking or sell personal information.

Local kitchen data

Inventory, groceries, recipes, plans, history, profile preferences, source settings, images, caches, activity, and exports are stored on your Mac. A bundled Python parser processes supported recipe markup locally.

Recipe sites

Browsing and imports download pages, feeds, sitemaps, and images from sources you select. Publishers receive normal request information such as IP address, time, user agent, and URL under their policies.

Household and AI service

When configured, household sync can send a household code, member/device identifier, display name, inventory, groceries, recipes, plans, changes, and deletion markers to the Stocked Cloudflare service. Intelligent fallback may send readable recipe text, URLs, kitchen data, preferences, or prompts to the Worker and Anthropic. Operational request data may appear in Cloudflare observability.

Deletion

Use clear controls or remove StockedMac Application Support data to erase one Mac. This does not automatically erase a shared household or other devices. Request server deletion at support@sowensstudios.com with the household/member identifier.
