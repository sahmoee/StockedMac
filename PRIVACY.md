# StockedMac Privacy Policy

**Effective and last updated: August 8, 2026**

StockedMac is a kitchen inventory, grocery, recipe, meal-planning, and recipe-research app for macOS from Sowens Studios. It contains no advertising SDK and does not use information for cross-app tracking or sell personal information.

## Information stored on your Mac

StockedMac stores inventory, groceries, recipes, saved recipes, meal plans, cooking history, profile and dietary preferences, source configuration, imported images, discovery caches, activity logs, and app settings in Application Support and preferences. Export files remain wherever you save them. The bundled recipe parser processes supported page markup locally on your Mac.

## Recipe browsing and imports

When you browse, test, or import a recipe source, StockedMac connects to the website or feed you selected and may download pages, sitemaps, feeds, and images. The publisher and its network providers can receive your IP address, request time, user-agent string, and requested URL under their own policies. Imported recipe text, source URL, and images may be saved locally.

## Stocked Worker, household sync, and AI

When configured, StockedMac connects to the Stocked service at `api.sowensstudios.com`. Household sync may transmit a household code, member/device identifier, display name, inventory, groceries, recipes, meal plans, changes, and deletion markers so invited devices can share the same kitchen. The service may store snapshots, changes, rate-limit records, and operational logs in Cloudflare infrastructure.

If local parsing cannot complete a requested import or you use an intelligent feature, readable recipe text, URLs, inventory, recipes, preferences, or the prompt needed for that feature may be sent to the Stocked Worker and then to its configured AI provider, currently Anthropic. Cloudflare observability and Anthropic may process request content and metadata under their policies.

## Diagnostics and support

Local activity and support reports can include source URLs, parser results, counts, versions, and error messages. They leave the Mac only when you copy or send them. Support email contains the address, message, and attachments you provide and is retained only as reasonably needed to respond, prevent abuse, and comply with law.

## Retention and deletion

Local information remains until you remove it, use StockedMac's clear controls, delete its Application Support folder, or uninstall the app. Clearing one Mac does not automatically delete a shared household's server data or data on other devices. To request deletion of server-held household information or support correspondence, email [support@sowensstudios.com](mailto:support@sowensstudios.com) with the household/member identifier shown in Settings where available.

External recipe sites, Cloudflare, Anthropic, and other services retain information under their own policies. Limited records may remain temporarily in backups, security logs, caches, or synchronization tombstones.

## Security, children, and changes

StockedMac uses HTTPS, authenticated Worker requests, macOS file protections, and rate limits where configured. No system is completely secure; protect exports and household invitations. StockedMac is a general-audience utility and is not directed to children under 13.

We may update this policy as functionality changes and will revise the date above. Privacy questions: [support@sowensstudios.com](mailto:support@sowensstudios.com).
